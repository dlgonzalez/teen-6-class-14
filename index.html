📡 Grammar Reported Speech ~20 min

🗣️ What is reported speech?

When you repeat what someone else said, you're using reported speech. You're not quoting word-for-word — you're reporting the message. This means you need to change the tenses, pronouns, and sometimes time expressions.

🎙️ Direct Speech — exact words
"I am really tired today."
The exact words, in quotation marks. The verb is in the PRESENT.
→
📋 Reported Speech — your summary
She said (that) she was really tired that day.
The verb moved to the PAST. The time word changed too.
The "that" rule: You can include or leave out "that" after say/tell — both are correct! "She said (that) she was tired." ✅

🆚 SAY vs TELL — the most common mistake

SAY 🗣️
After say you do NOT need to mention who you said it to. If you include a person, use to.
✅
She said she was hungry.
✅
He said to me that he was leaving.
❌
She said me she was hungry.
🧠 Pattern: say + (that) + clause
TELL 👤
After tell you ALWAYS need to say who you told. No exceptions!
✅
She told me she was hungry.
✅
He told the class he was leaving.
❌
She told she was hungry.
🧠 Pattern: tell + person + (that) + clause
Special tell phrases: tell the truth · tell a lie · tell a story · tell time · tell the difference — these don't use reported speech rules!

⏪ Tense Backshift — the time machine rule

When you report speech, the verbs "go back" one step in time. Think of it like pressing rewind on the tense. ⏪

🎙️ Direct Speech (original tense) 📋 Reported Speech (backshifted)
present simple
"I live in Cali."
 past simple
She said she lived in Cali.
present continuous
"We are watching TV."
 past continuous
They said they were watching TV.
present perfect
"I have seen that film."
 past perfect
He said he had seen that film.
past simple
"I called you."
 past perfect
She said she had called me.
will
"I will help you."
 would
He said he would help me.
can
"She can speak French."
 could
He said she could speak French.
must / have to
"I must leave now."
 had to
She said she had to leave.
🔄 Also change: Pronouns + Time expressions +
Pronouns change
I → he/she  |  we → they
my → his/her  |  you → I/they
"I lost my phone." → She said she had lost her phone.
Time expressions change
now → then  |  today → that day
tomorrow → the next day
yesterday → the day before
here → there  |  this → that
